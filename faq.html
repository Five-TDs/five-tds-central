UNITS & STATISTICS

Understanding unit information, damage, and calculations.

What does HPS stand for?

HPS stands for Hits Per Second. It measures how many times a unit attacks each second. For example, a unit with 2 HPS attacks twice per second.

What is the difference between DPS and HPS?

HPS (Hits Per Second) measures how frequently a unit attacks, while DPS (Damage Per Second) measures how much damage a unit deals over time. Both help determine a unit's offensive performance.

How is DPS calculated?

Raw DPS is calculated by multiplying a unit's damage by its Hits Per Second (HPS). Actual performance may vary depending on other factors.

Does having higher DPS automatically make a unit better?

Not necessarily! A unit's effectiveness depends on several factors, including range, placement cost, and usefulness in different situations.

Why does a unit have different stats in-game compared to the website?

Some statistics may differ because of game updates, unit levels, upgrades, or other modifiers. We recommend checking that you're comparing the same conditions.

Why are some units missing from the database?

Some units may not have been added yet because their statistics are unavailable or still being verified. You can report missing units using our suggestion form.

What if a unit's information is incorrect?

Use the suggestion form below and select "Incorrect Information." Include the unit's name, what information is incorrect, and what you believe the correct information is.

What is the difference between raw DPS and actual performance?

Raw DPS measures calculated damage output, while actual performance can depend on abilities, targeting, range, enemy behavior, and the situation in which a unit is used.

TIERLISTS & META

How rankings work and what makes units effective.

What does META mean in Five TD's?

META generally refers to the units and strategies considered most effective in the current version of the game. The META can change when units are added, adjusted, or new strategies are discovered.

How are tierlists and META rankings decided?

Rankings reflect unit performance, usefulness, and effectiveness in the current game. They are community-oriented opinions and may change as the game evolves.

Are the tierlists based on personal opinions?

Tierlists are based on assessments of unit performance, usefulness, and the current META. Rankings are not official and may differ from other players' opinions.

Why do tierlist rankings change?

Rankings can change when units receive buffs or nerfs, new units are introduced, or players discover better strategies.

Why is a unit ranked lower than I expected?

Rankings consider more than just damage. Factors such as cost, range, abilities, and overall usefulness can affect a unit's placement.

Should I always use the highest-ranked units?

Not always. A balanced team with good synergy can sometimes perform better than a team consisting entirely of highly ranked units.

What is the difference between Tierlist and Best Towers?

The Tierlist compares units across different rankings, while Best Towers highlights units that are particularly useful or effective. Both are intended to help players make decisions.

What are team compositions?

Team compositions are combinations of units selected to work well together. Different combinations may be better suited for different strategies, game modes, or situations.

Can I suggest changes to the tierlist?

Absolutely! If you believe a unit deserves a different ranking, submit your suggestion and explain your reasoning. Feedback may be considered when reviewing rankings.
